Freelance gig: Product description writer (contract, remote)

A Lagos-based e-commerce company is hiring a freelance writer to
produce 20 product descriptions (150-200 words each) for its store.

Terms: fixed fee of $120 for the full set, agreed in writing before
work starts. Payment via the platform's escrow - funds are released
when the descriptions are delivered and approved. No fee is required
to apply, and no purchase is necessary at any stage.

Deadline: applications close on 20 November; the writing deadline is
10 days from the start date stated in the contract.

Eligibility: applicants must submit two writing samples through the
platform. Shortlisted writers will be announced on the platform,
and questions are answered in the platform's message thread.
